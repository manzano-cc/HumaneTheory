BDASH.

Business Development + Dashboard. The open-source control center for AI agents that run your outbound pipeline: enriching leads, qualifying fit, drafting outreach, and logging everything back to your CRM, with a human always in the approval loop.

Try the workflow builder ↓ Set up your own

Agent orchestration

Chain enrichment, qualification and drafting agents into one pipeline. Each agent has a scope, a schedule and a log, like a real team member.

Human approval gates

Agents propose, your reps decide. Nothing gets sent to a real prospect without a human clicking approve.

Native integrations

Reads and writes to the CRM, inbox and calendar you already use. No new system of record to maintain.

Part of AIGEA.

Humane Theory is the applied-systems arm of AIGEA, the Artificial Intelligence Governance & Ethics Agency. AIGEA researches how AI should be governed and used responsibly; Humane Theory builds the models, tools and systems that put those principles to work inside real companies, with a human always able to check what the AI did and why.
