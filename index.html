/About

Building the systems.

Designing and building practical AI systems: tools that check governance readiness, track real ROI, watch competitors, and run business-development workflows. Everything here is free to use while it's in early build.

01

One job per system

Each system solves one concrete problem instead of trying to be a general-purpose black box nobody can explain.

02

Governed by design

Every system follows the same governance checks it's built to test for in others, not bolted on after the fact.

03

Your data stays yours

Nothing you enter is sold, shared, or reused against you. Free to use while it's being built in the open.

How a system gets built

01
Spot the problem

Starting from AI use seen happening badly, in the wild, never from a sales pitch.

02
Build with checks in

Logging, human checkpoints, and a governance pass before a system is ever used for real.

03
Ship it as a lab

Released free and clearly marked as in progress, stress-tested by real people, not a demo reel.

04
Listen & rebuild

Fixing what breaks and retiring what doesn't earn its place. Nothing here is finished.

Part of AIGEA.

Humane Theory is the applied-systems arm of AIGEA, the Artificial Intelligence Governance & Ethics Agency. AIGEA researches how AI should be governed and used responsibly; Humane Theory builds the models, tools and systems that put those principles to work inside real companies, with a human always able to check what the AI did and why.
